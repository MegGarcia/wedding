The local currency is the Mauritian rupee. Cards are widely accepted at hotels and larger venues, but it’s worth carrying some cash for smaller shops, tips, and taxis.

Mauritius runs on 230V and uses mainly UK-style three-pin (Type G) outlets, so bring a universal adapter. This is an easy one to forget.

Both English and French are widely spoken, so you won’t run into a language barrier.
